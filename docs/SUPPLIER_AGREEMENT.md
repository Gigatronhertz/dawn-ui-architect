## Background

A. Karije is an online travel company that sells group trips to travellers through karije.com. Karije sells those trips as principal and is the merchant of record: travellers buy from Karije, pay Karije, and their contract is with Karije.

B. The Supplier is a travel and tour operator. Karije wishes to buy the Supplier's services so that the Supplier can run certain trips for Karije's customers, and the Supplier wishes to provide them, on the terms of this agreement.

## 1. Definitions

- **Trip:** a trip listed in a Trip Schedule, which the Supplier runs for Karije's Customers.
- **Customer:** a traveller who has bought a place on a Trip from Karije.
- **Net Rate:** the amount Karije pays the Supplier for each Customer who takes part in a Trip, as set out in the Trip Schedule.
- **Retail Price:** the price Karije charges its Customers for a Trip.
- **Trip Schedule:** a description of a Trip agreed in writing (including through the Karije Pro dashboard) in the form of Schedule 1.
- **Payout Account:** the Supplier's bank account, in the Supplier's own name, verified by Karije under clause 10.
- **Business Day:** a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in Lagos State.

## 2. Appointment

2.1 Karije appoints the Supplier, on a non-exclusive basis, to provide the services described in each Trip Schedule to Karije's Customers on Karije's behalf.

2.2 Karije sells each Trip in its own name and is responsible to its Customers for the Trip as described. The Supplier does not sell Trips to Customers and has no contract with them for the Trip.

2.3 Karije sets the Retail Price and the terms on which it sells Trips, including its cancellation and refund terms. The Supplier sets its Net Rate.

2.4 Nothing in this agreement makes either party the agent, partner or employee of the other, and neither may commit the other to anything except as set out here.

## 3. Trips

3.1 For each Trip, the Supplier provides a Trip Schedule giving at least: the itinerary, dates, meeting point and times; what is and isn't included; the minimum and maximum number of Customers; the Net Rate; and any requirements for Customers (for example age, fitness or documents).

3.2 The Supplier is responsible for the Trip Schedule being accurate and complete, and for telling Karije straight away if anything in it changes.

3.3 Karije may decide whether, when and how to offer a Trip for sale, and may stop selling it at any time.

3.4 Once a Customer has paid for a Trip, its Net Rate and contents can only change if both parties agree in writing.

## 4. The Supplier's obligations

The Supplier must:

- (a) run each Trip as set out in its Trip Schedule, with reasonable care and skill;
- (b) hold, and keep in force, every registration, licence and permit needed to run its Trips, and show copies to Karije on request;
- (c) use roadworthy, properly licensed and insured vehicles, and qualified, competent drivers, guides and staff;
- (d) follow all applicable laws and safety standards, and carry out a reasonable risk assessment for each activity;
- (e) treat Customers fairly and courteously, and tell Karije straight away about any accident, injury, serious complaint or incident on a Trip;
- (f) not ask for or accept any payment from a Customer for anything included in a Trip, and not offer Customers its own products or services for the Trip in place of Karije's;
- (g) give Karije, before each Trip, the name and phone number of the trip leader on the day.

## 5. Karije's obligations

Karije will:

- (a) market and sell Trips, collect payment from Customers, and handle Customer bookings, questions, changes, cancellations and refunds;
- (b) send the Supplier the list of Customers (names and phone numbers) for each Trip no later than [24] hours before it starts, and update it if it changes;
- (c) pay the Supplier under clause 6.

## 6. Payment

6.1 For each Trip, Karije pays the Supplier the Net Rate for each Customer who took part (or who did not turn up but was not entitled to a refund under Karije's terms).

6.2 Karije pays within [5] Business Days after the Trip has ended, provided the Trip was delivered as set out in its Trip Schedule and no complaint about it is open under clause 8. If a complaint is open, Karije may hold the amount affected until it is resolved.

6.3 Karije may, at its discretion and by written agreement for a particular Trip, pay part of the Net Rate before the Trip starts to cover the Supplier's costs. Any amount paid in advance is deducted from the final payment and must be repaid if the Trip does not go ahead.

6.4 All payments are made by bank transfer to the Payout Account only. Karije will not pay into any other account.

6.5 Karije's records of bookings and payments are the basis for calculating what is owed. The Supplier can ask for a statement for any Trip.

6.6 The Net Rate is inclusive of any VAT the Supplier must charge. Each party is responsible for its own taxes. Where the law requires Karije to deduct withholding tax, Karije will deduct it and give the Supplier the receipt.

6.7 Karije may deduct from any amount it owes the Supplier any amount the Supplier owes Karije under this agreement, including under clauses 7 and 9.

## 7. Changes and cancellations

7.1 **By the Supplier.** If the Supplier needs to change a significant part of a Trip, or cancel it, it must tell Karije in writing as soon as possible and in any case at least [7] days before the Trip starts (or straight away, if the reason arises later). Where the change or cancellation is the Supplier's fault, the Supplier must repay Karije any refunds, compensation and reasonable extra costs Karije pays its Customers as a result, and no Net Rate is payable for affected Customers.

7.2 **Not enough Customers.** If fewer than the minimum number of Customers have booked [5] days before the Trip starts, either party may cancel it by written notice, and neither owes the other anything for it, except that the Supplier repays any advance under clause 6.3.

7.3 **By Karije's Customers.** Customer cancellations are handled by Karije under its terms. Karije pays the Supplier only for Customers who take part, except where the Trip Schedule lists costs the Supplier cannot recover (for example a non-refundable booking), in which case Karije pays those costs for Customers who cancel late.

7.4 **Events beyond control.** Neither party is liable for failing to perform because of events outside its reasonable control (such as severe weather, road closures, strikes, civil unrest or government action). If such an event stops a Trip going ahead, neither party owes the other the Net Rate for it, and the Supplier repays any advance less its reasonable costs actually incurred and evidenced.

## 8. Complaints

8.1 Karije handles all Customer complaints. The Supplier must respond to Karije within [2] Business Days of being asked about a complaint and cooperate in resolving it.

8.2 If Karije reasonably finds that a Trip was not delivered as described because of the Supplier, the Supplier must repay Karije any refund or compensation Karije gives the Customer as a result, up to the Net Rate for that Customer, unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.

## 9. Liability, indemnity and insurance

9.1 The Supplier is responsible for the services it provides and for the acts and omissions of its staff, drivers, guides and subcontractors.

9.2 The Supplier indemnifies Karije against any claim, loss, cost or fine (including reasonable legal costs) arising from the Supplier's breach of this agreement, negligence, or failure to comply with the law in running a Trip.

9.3 The Supplier must hold, for as long as it runs Trips for Karije, public liability insurance of at least ₦[TO COMPLETE] per claim, and any insurance required by law for its vehicles and staff, and show evidence on request.

9.4 Except for clause 9.2, fraud, or death or personal injury caused by negligence, neither party is liable to the other for indirect or consequential loss or loss of profit, and each party's total liability to the other under this agreement in any 12 months is limited to the total Net Rate paid or payable in that period.

## 10. Verification, payouts and compliance

10.1 Before any Trip is offered for sale, the Supplier must give Karije: its business registration documents; the National Identification Number and an identity document of the person responsible for the business; proof of address; and details of the Payout Account.

10.2 The Payout Account must be in the Supplier's own name. Karije will verify it with the bank before the first payment.

10.3 The Supplier may change the Payout Account only by written request from its authorised representative. Karije will confirm the change by phone on the number given in the Supplier's original application before paying into the new account, and may hold payments until it has done so.

10.4 The Supplier must tell Karije within [5] Business Days of any change to its ownership, directors, business details or contact details.

10.5 The Supplier confirms that it, its owners and its directors are not on any sanctions list, and will tell Karije straight away if that changes.

10.6 Each party must comply with the Money Laundering (Prevention and Prohibition) Act 2022, the Terrorism (Prevention and Prohibition) Act 2022 and other applicable anti-money-laundering and anti-corruption laws. Neither party may offer, give or accept any bribe or improper payment in connection with this agreement. The Supplier must provide any information Karije reasonably needs to meet its own obligations under those laws and its bank's requirements.

## 11. Personal data

11.1 The Supplier receives Customers' personal data from Karije only so that it can run the Trip. It must use that data only for that purpose, keep it secure and confidential, not share it except as needed to run the Trip, and delete it within [30] days after the Trip ends unless the law requires it to be kept.

11.2 Each party must comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, and tell the other within 48 hours of becoming aware of any breach affecting Customers' data.

## 12. Customers and non-circumvention

12.1 Customers who book through Karije are Karije's customers. During this agreement and for [12] months after it ends, the Supplier must not use Customers' details to market to them or to sell them trips directly, unless the Customer comes to the Supplier independently.

## 13. Brand and content

13.1 The Supplier allows Karije to use its name, logo, and the text and photos it provides, to describe and market the Trips. The Supplier confirms it has the right to give that permission.

13.2 The Supplier may say that its trips are sold on Karije but must not otherwise use Karije's name or brand without Karije's written consent.

## 14. Confidentiality

Each party must keep confidential the other's business information that it receives under this agreement, including Net Rates and Customer information, and use it only for this agreement, except where disclosure is required by law.

## 15. Term and termination

15.1 This agreement starts on the date above and continues until either party ends it by giving [30] days' written notice.

15.2 Either party may end it immediately by written notice if the other: seriously breaches it and (where the breach can be fixed) does not fix it within [7] days of being asked to; becomes insolvent; or, in the Supplier's case, endangers Customers' safety or fails verification under clause 10.

15.3 When this agreement ends, the Supplier must still run any Trip for which Customers have already paid, unless Karije tells it otherwise, and Karije must still pay for Trips delivered. Clauses 6, 9, 11, 12, 14 and 17 continue after it ends.

## 16. Karije Pro

If the Supplier subscribes to Karije Pro, the subscription is governed by Karije's Terms of Service and is separate from this agreement. Ending the subscription does not end this agreement, and ending this agreement does not end the subscription.

## 17. Disputes and governing law

17.1 This agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

17.2 The parties will first try to settle any dispute by discussion between senior representatives within [14] days. If that fails, either party may refer it to mediation at the Lagos Multi-Door Courthouse, and if mediation does not settle it within [30] days, to the courts of Lagos State.

18.2 Changes to this agreement are only valid if made in writing and signed by both parties. Trip Schedules may be agreed through the Karije Pro dashboard.

18.3 The Supplier may not transfer or subcontract any of its obligations without Karije's written consent, except to its own drivers and guides.
